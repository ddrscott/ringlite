## Target Audience
YouTubers, Twitch streamers, course creators, podcasters, and social media content creators who need consistent lighting across all their content.

## The Problem (Villain)
Physical ring lights are a pain—they don't fit in your bag, need constant charging, and worst of all, they show up in your screen recordings and streams.

### Pain Points
- Ring lights visible in screen recordings and OBS captures
- Can't travel with bulky lighting equipment
- Battery dies mid-stream or mid-recording
- Inconsistent lighting when shooting in different locations
- Desk clutter from tripods and stands
- Expensive professional lighting setups

### Emotional Stakes
- Frustration when ring light shows up in final export
- Anxiety about lighting consistency across content
- Overwhelm from managing too much gear
- Embarrassment when production quality suffers on the road

## The Transformation (Success)
Studio-quality lighting that travels in your laptop. Consistent look across all content—home studio, hotel room, or coffee shop. And it never shows up in your recordings.

## Key Statistics
- Average content creator travels 4-6x per year for events/collabs
- Screen recording is a primary content format for tutorials, courses, and gaming
- Portable ring lights cost $50-150 and still have the visibility problem
