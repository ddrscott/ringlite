## Target Audience
Sales professionals, consultants, and business travelers who take video calls from hotels, airports, coffee shops, and client sites.

## The Problem (Villain)
Bad lighting in unfamiliar locations destroys credibility before you even speak. You're closing deals on video, but you look like you're calling from a cave.

### Pain Points
- Hotel room lighting is universally terrible
- Can't pack a ring light in your carry-on
- No time to set up equipment between calls
- First impressions happen in the first 7 seconds
- Looking unprofessional costs deals

### Emotional Stakes
- Anxiety before important client calls
- Embarrassment when you're the dark square in the meeting
- Fear of losing credibility with prospects
- Frustration with gear that doesn't travel well

## The Transformation (Success)
Walk into any room, open your laptop, and look like you're in a professional studio. Confidence on every call, anywhere in the world.

## Key Statistics
- Deals are 127% more likely to close when video is used
- First impressions form in 7 seconds
- 50% of sales teams using video improved close rates

## StoryBrand Framework

### 1. Character (Hero)
A sales professional who lives on video calls and travels constantly

### 2. Problem
- **External:** Bad lighting in hotels/travel locations
- **Internal:** Feels unprofessional, anxious about first impressions
- **Philosophical:** You shouldn't need a production studio to look credible

### 3. Guide (RingLite)
- **Empathy:** "We've all been the dark square in the meeting"
- **Authority:** Native macOS app, invisible to screen share, used by remote professionals

### 4. Plan
1. Download RingLite (free, 10 uses)
2. Position the ring around your camera
3. Look professional on every call

### 5. Call to Action
"Download Free" / "Look Pro on Every Call"

### 6. Success
- Confident on calls from anywhere
- Professional first impressions every time
- No gear to pack, charge, or set up
- Close more deals

### 7. Failure
- Continue looking washed out and tired
- Lose credibility in the first 7 seconds
- Miss opportunities because of bad optics
