## Target Audience
Full-time remote employees, hybrid workers, and anyone who spends significant time on Zoom/Teams/Meet calls from their home office.

## The Problem (Villain)
You look tired on camera—even when you're not. Bad lighting makes everyone look washed out, and you're sick of seeing your own exhausted face staring back at you all day.

### Pain Points
- Always look tired/washed out on camera regardless of how you feel
- Physical ring lights clutter the desk
- Ring lights need charging (and die mid-call)
- Constant self-view fatigue—seeing yourself look bad all day
- Tried "natural light" solutions but they're inconsistent
- Don't want to buy another gadget

### Emotional Stakes
- Self-consciousness about appearance on calls
- Dread before turning on camera
- Frustration with home office lighting setup
- Feeling less confident than in-person

## The Transformation (Success)
Look refreshed and professional on every call without thinking about it. No more checking your self-view and cringing. No more desk clutter.

## Key Statistics
- Remote workers spend 4+ hours daily on video calls
- "Camera fatigue" is a documented phenomenon—seeing yourself look bad increases stress
- Women and newer employees feel disproportionate pressure to look good on camera

## StoryBrand Framework

A remote worker who's on camera daily and tired of looking tired

- **External:** Bad home office lighting makes them look washed out

### 3. Guide (RingLite)
- **Empathy:** "We get it—you're tired of looking tired"
- **Authority:** Lightweight native app, used by thousands of remote workers, invisible to screen share

### 4. Plan
1. Download RingLite (free)
2. Position around your camera
3. Forget about lighting forever

### 5. Call to Action
"Download Free" / "Stop Looking Tired"

### 6. Success
- Look refreshed without effort
- No desk clutter
- No charging, no setup
- Actually feel confident on camera

### 7. Failure
- Continue looking washed out daily
- Keep dreading camera-on meetings
- Buy another gadget that doesn't solve the problem

## Ad Creative Direction

### Image Concept 1: Before/After Face
Split or side-by-side: Same person, same room
Left: Overhead lighting, shadows under eyes, washed out, looking exhausted
Right: Even soft lighting, looking alert and professional

### Image Concept 2: The Desk Problem
Cluttered desk with physical ring light, charging cable, stands
vs.
Clean minimal desk with just laptop, same great lighting

### Image Concept 3: The Self-View
Zoom self-view window showing washed out face with sad/frustrated expression
vs.
Same window, well-lit face, confident expression
Caption: "Your self-view doesn't have to hurt"

## Landing Page Structure

1. **Hero Section**
   - Headline: "Stop looking tired on Zoom"

   - CTA: Download Free (macOS)

2. **Problem Agitation**
   - "You've tried natural light. You've bought a ring light. You still look like a ghost on camera."
   - Address the frustration: desk clutter, charging, inconsistent results

3. **Solution Introduction**
   - RingLite: Perfect lighting that lives in your laptop
   - No hardware, no charging, no clutter
   - Invisible to screen recordings

4. **How It Works**
   - 3 steps: Download, position, forget
   - Screenshot/GIF of the app in action

5. **Problem Comparison**
   - "Why physical ring lights fail"

6. **Pricing**

7. **Final CTA**
   - "Download now. Look refreshed on your next call."
